# Included music

The prototype now includes actual Roblox Creator Store audio IDs, crossfades, volume control, reverb and a gradually slowing/muffling dream soundtrack.

- **Dream State (c)** — 9044720271, APM Music; the dream theme and a quiet pitched-down dread layer. https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/9044720271/Dream-State-c
- **Unwrapping the Presents** — 1837256134, APM Music; a quiet music-box bedroom theme. https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/1837256134/Unwrapping-the-Presents
- **Cursed Times Piano Ping** — 9038125533, APMOfficial; the awakening sting. https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/9038125533/Cursed-Times-Piano-Ping

Listings were checked on Roblox's Creator Store. Playback still depends on Roblox asset availability and the experience's permissions. If Roblox blocks an asset, replace its ID in Config with an audio asset your experience can use. Audio is client-local and never blocks story progression while loading.
